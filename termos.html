Terms of Service — Games News Drafts

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Games News Drafts is a private tool used only by its owner to upload short game news videos, produced by the owner, as drafts to the owner's own TikTok account. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

By authorizing the app, the account owner allows it to send video files to the TikTok draft inbox of that account. Nothing is published automatically: every video is reviewed and published manually by the owner in the TikTok app. The tool is provided "as is", without warranties. Access can be revoked at any time in TikTok > Settings and privacy > Security > Manage app permissions.
